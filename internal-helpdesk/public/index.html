Frequently Asked Questions (Based on Feedback)

Q: How quickly will my Urgent priority ticket be addressed?
Urgent priority tickets carry a strict 4-hour SLA. If unresolved within 4 hours, the ticket console flags an SLA BREACH warning to senior IT admins automatically.
Q: Who receives my ticket when I pick a Routing Queue?
Selecting an Input Handler (IT Team, HR, SAP Support, or Other) routes your ticket directly to the specialized queue monitored by dedicated teams.
Q: How do I attach multiple screenshots or files?
Under the Raise Ticket form, use the optional 'Attachments' input field. You can hold Ctrl (or Cmd) and select multiple files simultaneously before submitting.
Q: What does 'On-Hold' status mean?
On-Hold indicates that resolution is pending external vendor dependencies, employee response, or scheduled maintenance windows.
